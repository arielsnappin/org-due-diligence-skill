# Prep your next conversation (Coach) → Call Sheet

**Goal:** the user walks into their next call knowing the 3–5 things worth asking, what a real answer sounds like, and how to handle the tricky moments. One page, about five minutes.

## Frame it (ask only what you don't already know)

- **Who is the call with, and when?** Identify their role in the decision:
  - recruiter (gatekeeper and logistics)
  - hiring manager (the decider and future boss)
  - skip-level or functional leader
  - founder or CEO
  - peer
  - prospective report
  - backchannel contact
- **What does the user most want to walk away knowing?** Aim for 1–3 things. If Compare has already run, start from its decision-critical unknowns.

If the org hasn't been read yet, spend at most 5 minutes on a quick scan: the person's title, time in seat, prior roles, and recent public posts, plus the company basics. Tell the user the questions are less targeted as a result.

## Build the sheet

1. **Walk-away goals** (1–3).
2. **3–5 questions**, in priority order: decision-critical unknowns first, then the biggest gaps on ★ dimensions.
   - Pull from `references/question-bank.md` for this conversation type.
   - Tailor each question with what you know (names, facts from the dossier).
   - Phrase them so they're safe to ask at this stage: curious, not accusatory.
3. **Listen-fors** for each question: what a real answer sounds like versus a non-answer. For example, a real answer to "What's the path to Director?" names a cycle, specific gaps, and a date. A non-answer sounds like "You're doing great, let's revisit."
4. **When to ask.** Tag anything timing-sensitive as before the loop, during, at the offer, or after signing. Some asks cost nothing early and a lot late; asking to be considered at a higher level is the classic example.
5. **Tricky moments.** Where relevant, include the matching script from `references/scripts.md`:
   - asking for more time
   - asking to be considered at a higher level
   - asking about the predecessor
   - mentioning a competing offer
   - the "stay" conversation with the current manager
6. **At the offer stage.** List what to clarify or get in writing: title, level, reporting line, scope, team and headcount, location policy, and start date.

## Output

Use `templates/call-sheet.md`. If you can write files, save it as `call-sheet-<who>-<date>.md` in the run folder.

Close with: "After the call, paste your notes (messy is fine) and I'll update the picture."
