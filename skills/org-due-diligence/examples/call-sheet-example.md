# Call Sheet: the founder-CEO of Company A
*Illustrative example: every company, person, and number here is fictional. Thursday, 4pm · The founder's role in your decision: this conversation decides it · ★ = path to the next level; scope & team*

## Walk away knowing
1. **Which product decisions** the founder will hand over, and when.
2. **What happened with the previous Head of Product**, who left after 11 months.
3. **The office policy**, if the recruiter hasn't answered by email.

## About them (30 seconds)
Founder-CEO for five years. Before that, Head of Product at a large analytics company. Still runs the weekly product reviews and said on a 2025 podcast that they read every spec [Reported · podcast]. Three of the four engineering leads came from their previous company [Reported · LinkedIn].

## Questions, in priority order
| # | Ask | Why it matters | Listen for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Which product decisions did you make last month that you'd want me to make three months from now?" | ★ Scope and path: the job is only real if the founder lets go | ✅ Names specific decisions and a handover plan · ⚠️ "We'll figure it out together" |
| 2 | "What happened with the previous Head of Product, and what would you do differently this time?" | ★ Path: a lateral exit after 11 months usually means the job wasn't what was promised | ✅ Owns part of it and names a change · ⚠️ Blames the person, or "it wasn't a fit" |
| 3 | "Have you talked with [the acting product lead] about this hire? How do you see their role?" | Scope: a passed-over internal candidate can make your first six months hard | ✅ Yes, with a plan for them · ⚠️ "They'll be fine" |
| 4 | "What would make you say, a year from now, that this hire was a success?" | ★ Path: whether "grows into Head of Product" has criteria | ✅ Concrete outcomes and a review date · ⚠️ Only vibes |
| 5 | "What's the office policy for the product team?" | Must-have: 3 office days max | ✅ A clear number · ⚠️ "We like people in a lot" |

⏱ **Timing note:** ask question 1 first, while the founder is still selling. After you sign, the same question becomes a negotiation.

## If it comes up
**"Where else are you talking?"**
> "I have an offer in hand and I'm taking the decision seriously. This is the conversation that matters most to me."

Be honest and never bluff about a deadline. Founders talk to each other.

## At the offer: get it in writing
- Title, reporting line, and the exec-team seat
- The criteria and a review date for Head of Product
- Whether design reports to you
- The office policy
