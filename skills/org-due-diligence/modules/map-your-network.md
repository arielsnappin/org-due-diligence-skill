# Map your network (Researcher) → Backchannel List

**Goal:** find the few people who can tell the user the truth about this org, especially about the hiring manager, and make reaching out easy. Backchannel replies take days, so this is worth starting early.

This module needs LinkedIn access (see `modules/intake.md`). Without it, give the user the three searches to run themselves:
1. 1st-degree connections at the company.
2. 1st-degree connections who used to work there.
3. People who've worked with the hiring manager.

Then offer to rank whatever they paste back.

## Steps

1. **Find the company on LinkedIn** and its numeric company ID (see `references/research-playbook.md`).
2. **Current employees they know:** 1st-degree connections at the company.
3. **Former employees they know:** 1st-degree connections who used to work there. These are often the most candid.
4. **People close to the hiring manager:** anyone who has worked with or for them, at this company or a previous one. The best read on a manager usually comes from a former report.
5. **2nd-degree paths:** for the most valuable people the user *doesn't* know, note the mutual connection. A warm intro beats a cold note.

Browse at human pace. You need a shortlist, not a census.

## Rank

| Tier | Who |
|---|---|
| **1** | Worked with the hiring manager or on this role's team, and left or joined recently |
| **2** | In the same function, and recent |
| **3** | Elsewhere in the company, or left more than ~2 years ago |

Say *why* each person made the list, in a few words. Relationship strength matters, and only the user knows it, so ask them to star the people they'd actually message. Keep the list to 5–8 people.

## Draft notes

For each Tier 1–2 person, draft a short note (at most 80 words) that the user can send themselves. Each note has:
- a warm, specific opener (how they know each other, or who the mutual connection is);
- one clear ask: a 15-minute call, or a couple of answers by message;
- 2–3 tailored questions from the Backchannel section of `references/question-bank.md`;
- an easy out ("totally fine if the timing doesn't work").

Never ask for anything confidential. For 2nd-degree people, draft the intro request to the mutual connection instead.

**You never send, connect, or message.** The user does that. Keep the list private, because it names real people in the user's network.

## Output

Use `templates/backchannel-list.md`. If you can write files, save it as `backchannel-<company>.md` in the run folder.

In chat, show the ranked list (name, connection, why), then: "Star the ones you'd actually message and I'll tailor those notes." Suggest one next step.
