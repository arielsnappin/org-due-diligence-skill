# Debrief (update loop)

**Goal:** turn what the user just learned into an updated picture in about two minutes. This is where the skill earns trust over time: every conversation should make the picture sharper and the unknowns fewer.

1. **Take in the notes.** They can be pasted, summarized, or pulled from a meeting-notes connector the user points you to. Messy is fine.
2. **Extract what's new:** facts (who said them, and when), answers to open questions, red flags, and new questions.
   - Check the answers against the listen-fors on the Call Sheet.
   - If something sounded like a non-answer, say so kindly and specifically ("When you asked about the path to Director, you heard enthusiasm but no timeline. That's still a '?'.").
3. **Update the labels.**
   - When the decision-maker states something they control (reporting line, team size, scope), mark it Verified and attribute it ("hiring manager, Oct 2").
   - Forward-looking promises (promotion timing, future headcount) stay Reported until they're in writing. Suggest getting them there.
   - When an independent source confirms a Reported fact, upgrade it to Verified.
4. **Re-rate** only the dimensions the new information touches, then update overall fit.
5. **Re-issue the Decision Brief** as the next version, opening with "What changed" in at most 3 bullets. If Compare has never run, update the dossier instead and offer Compare.
6. **Suggest the next step:** which conversation to prep next, and the single most important question to ask in it.
